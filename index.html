Threat Intelligence Aggregator

A Python-based cybersecurity tool that collects, parses, normalizes, correlates, and reports threat indicators (IOCs) from multiple feeds.

Project Overview

Security teams receive threat intelligence from multiple sources such as OSINT feeds, security tools, and CERT alerts. These feeds come in different formats and require normalization and correlation to be useful.

This project automates that workflow by ingesting IOC feeds, correlating repeated indicators, and generating deployment-ready blocklists.

Pipeline

Threat Feeds ↓ Load Feeds ↓ Parse Indicators ↓ Normalize Data ↓ Correlation Engine ↓ Generate Blocklists ↓ Threat Intelligence Report

Modules

  • parser.py – Parses CSV, TXT, JSON feeds
  • normalizer.py – Cleans and validates indicators
  • correlator.py – Correlates indicators across feeds
  • blocklist_gen.py – Generates blocklist files
  • reporter.py – Produces final threat intelligence reports

Example Output

Feeds processed: 4 Raw IOCs collected: 57 Unique indicators: 35 Cross-feed correlations: 13 High-risk indicators: 3 Blocklist files generated: 8
